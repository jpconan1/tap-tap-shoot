WEBSOCKET ORIGIN ENFORCEMENT REMINDER

Before the public release:

1. Open the Render dashboard and review the [ws-origin] summaries in the service logs.
2. Test every legitimate client, including the Render website and the itch.io build.
3. Add legitimate origins reported under wouldReject to WS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS.
4. Investigate unexpected origins. Do not add an origin unless you know which legitimate client produced it.
5. When the allowlist is confirmed, change this Render environment variable:

   WS_ORIGIN_MODE=enforce

6. Redeploy or restart the Render service.
7. Retest every supported client and confirm legitimate connections still work.

Until WS_ORIGIN_MODE is set to enforce, unexpected browser origins are only logged. They are not blocked.
