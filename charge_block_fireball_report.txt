Charge Block Fireball Nash Report

Rules modeled
- Players start at 1 bar each.
- Charge gives +1 bar.
- Reaching 3 bars wins.
- If both players reach 3 bars at same time, both reset to 2 bars.
- Fireball costs 1 bar.
- Fireball beats charge and ends game immediately.
- Block draws fireball and game continues.
- Fireball always costs 1 bar when chosen, even if blocked.

Terms
- Value: your win chance from that state if both players play perfectly.
- Nash mix: the move spread a perfect player should use.
- CPU mix: the Nash mix for the opponent in that state.

Read
- Perfect play makes the starting game fair.
- A 2-bar lead over 1 bar is strong, worth about 74% win chance.
- Being down 1 bar to 2 bars is bad, but still live at about 26%.
- Being at 0 bars against 2 bars is checkmate loss.
- Being at 2 bars against 0 bars is checkmate win.
- Strong-player games end slightly more often by fireball kill than by 3-bar victory.

Math confidence
- Confidence is high, assuming the listed rules are correct.
- The game is modeled as a finite zero-sum simultaneous-reveal stochastic game.
- Each state is solved as a minimax/Nash mixed strategy.
- Symmetry checks pass: mirrored states add to 100%, and equal states are 50%.
- Solver converged in 25 iterations.
- Some checkmate states have non-unique CPU mixes, but the state values are stable.
