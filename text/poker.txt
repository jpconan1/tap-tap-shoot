RPS POKER - CURRENT RULES

CORE IDEA

RPS Poker combines hidden Rock Paper Scissors choices with Poker betting.
Each player owns a stack of real chips. Chips won and lost persist throughout
the match.


MATCH GOAL

- Each player begins with 9 chips.
- There are 18 chips total.
- A player loses when their stack reaches 0.
- A player also loses if they cannot pay the next scheduled ante.
- The surviving player wins the match.


HAND SETUP

1. Both players pay the ante.
2. Both players secretly lock Rock, Paper, or Scissors.
3. One random community move is revealed publicly.
4. Each locked move receives a strength by resolving against the community.
5. The players bet without seeing the opponent's locked move.

The community move is revealed only after both moves are locked. Revealing it
first would let both players choose the strongest move every hand.


SHOWDOWN RANKING

- A move that beats the community is High.
- A move that ties the community is Middle.
- A move that loses to the community is Low.
- High beats Middle.
- Middle beats Low.
- Equal strengths tie.

Example with Rock as the community:

Locked move    Result against Rock    Strength
Paper          Win                    High
Rock           Tie                    Middle
Scissors       Loss                   Low

Every move has an equal chance to become High, Middle, or Low before the
community is revealed.


ANTES

The ante increases only after both players have acted first at the current
ante:

Hand 1: ante 1
Hand 2: ante 1
Hand 3: ante 2
Hand 4: ante 2
Hand 5: ante 3
Hand 6: ante 3

The pattern continues 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, and so on.

- The first actor is random on Hand 1.
- The first actor alternates every hand.
- Each player therefore acts first once before the ante increases.
- The ante advances after every second hand, including after tied hands.
- A player who cannot pay the full scheduled ante loses immediately.
- There is no standard-RPS tiebreaker or forced-RPS phase.

Rare equal-stack edge:

- If both players are below the scheduled ante and their stacks are unequal,
  the shorter stack loses.
- If both players have equal stacks below the scheduled ante, both put their
  entire stacks into a normal RPS Poker hand.
- This all-in hand still uses locked moves, a community move, and Poker
  showdown ranking. It does not use standard RPS.


BETTING

The available actions are check, bet, call, raise, and fold.

- Bets use whole chips.
- Minimum opening bet: 1 chip.
- No player may wager more than the opponent can match.
- Unmatched excess never enters the pot.
- A normal raise must increase the wager by at least the previous betting
  increment.
- A player may move all-in for less than the normal minimum raise.
- Betting ends on a call or fold.
- Two checks also end betting and cause a showdown.


HAND RESOLUTION

Fold:

- The opponent takes the pot.
- Locked moves remain secret.
- The folding player's card leaves the table.

Called wager:

- Both locked moves are revealed.
- The stronger result takes the entire pot.
- Equal results split the pot evenly.

Check-check:

- Both locked moves are revealed.
- The stronger result takes the ante pot.
- Equal results split the ante pot evenly.

All contributions are matched. A tied showdown therefore restores the players'
pre-hand stacks exactly.


PRESENTATION FLOW

Every hand:

- The table deals a facedown card before players choose their locked moves.
- The deal animation repeats at the start of every hand.

Fold:

- Pot chips disappear one at a time and enter the winner's stack.
- The folded card and community card clear with the choppy falling animation.

Check-check winner:

- Pot chips disappear one at a time and enter the winner's stack.
- Revealed cards clear with the choppy falling animation.
- The Poker winner cutaway is not used.

Any tied showdown:

- Pot chips disappear one at a time.
- Chips alternate between the two stacks.
- Revealed cards clear with the choppy falling animation.
- There is no spike wipe between hands.

Called-wager winner:

- Cards reveal and hold for 3 beats.
- The game spike-wipes to the Poker winner scene.
- The scene flips when Player 2 wins.
- The winner scene holds for 4 beats.
- If the match continues, the game spike-wipes to the empty table and deals
  the next hand.

Match end:

- The game spike-wipes to the shared game-won or game-lost scene.
- Continue begins a rematch.
- Quit returns to the menu.


MUSIC

- RPS Poker begins with the normal game loops.
- The final/tiebreaker topper is not active at match start.
- The topper begins only when at least one player's remaining stack is below
  the next hand's scheduled ante.
