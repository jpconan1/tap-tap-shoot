Punch Stab Shoot Nash Report

Rules modeled
- Players start at 3 HP each.
- Punch beats shoot and deals 1 damage.
- Shoot beats stab and deals 3 damage.
- Stab beats punch and deals 2 damage.
- Damage only happens when a player wins the matchup.
- Ties deal no damage and the game continues.

Terms
- Value: your win chance from that HP state if both players play perfectly.
- Nash mix: the move spread a perfect player should use.
- CPU mix: the Nash mix for the opponent in that state.

Starting state
- Start: You 3 HP, CPU 3 HP.
- Game value: 50%.
- Perfect CPU mix: punch 59%, stab 14%, shoot 28%.

Read
- Perfect play makes the starting game fair.
- Punch is the most common opening move, even though it deals the least damage.
- Stab is used least from most states because shoot kills it for 3 damage.
- Shoot is still common because it can instantly kill any opponent at 3 HP or less.
- At equal 1 HP, the game collapses to plain rock-paper-scissors because any winning hit kills.
- Having 3 HP against 1 HP is strong, worth about 74% win chance.
- Being at 1 HP against 3 HP is weak, but still live at about 26%.

Math confidence
- Confidence is high, assuming the listed rules are correct.
- The game is modeled as a finite zero-sum simultaneous-reveal stochastic game.
- Each state is solved as a minimax/Nash mixed strategy.
- Symmetry checks pass: mirrored states add to 100%, and equal states are 50%.
- Solver converged in 33 iterations.
